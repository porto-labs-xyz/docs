## Current direction

The classical past is the foundation, expressed through spare architectural pencil studies. The foreground represents the system being built, with stronger vector outlines, measured hatching, and restrained red circuitry. Dark charcoal fades gently toward warm light. Maintain calm, rounded translucent panels, generous space, and restrained typography.

- Keep the deck at 15 slides.
- Keep the pillar centred on slide 5 and the Venn diagram offset to its right.
- Alternate left and right background compositions.
- Avoid instruments, maritime imagery, particles, astrology, ornate machinery, neon, and game-like CGI.
- Keep the monospaced headings small. The latest reduction was 16% for the large mono headings.
- Preserve copy unless a specific revision is requested.
- Match foreground chart geometry to the original data, with stronger line weight and hatching.
- Preserve the circular circulation symbol in the second donut. An open padlock beside it contrasts with the first donut's closed lock.
- The existing round slide uses the empty upper area for a small capital-to-network-to-artists drawing. It is a conceptual use-of-investment illustration, not a financial return projection.

## Slide 5, current narrative

The artist and the distributor
should be the same party.

We now have the means and technology for artists to own and run the infrastructure that delivers their music.

The boundary between artist and distributor is an inherited limitation. It keeps distribution value away from the people who create the IP and musical works.

No more moats where none are needed.

## Final image prompt

Create a widescreen 16:9 minimalist architectural pencil drawing on near black charcoal paper for a refined investor presentation. Subject: two fine parallel infrastructure channels, like abstract circuit rails carved in a simple classical stone foundation, running diagonally from lower right toward centre right. Three small open square nodes sit along the rails, a single restrained red pencil route flows along them into a small outlined square reservoir, expressing infrastructure carrying and retaining value. NOT literal railway tracks, no trains, coins, symbols, currency signs or text. Fine warm grey graphite contours, selective subtle crosshatching on one side of the low foundation, faint construction lines. No shaded solid CGI surfaces, no game, no neon, no instruments. A spare unfinished architect's sketch, elegant elementary classical geometry. Artwork confined to RIGHT 32% and between 28% and 73% height. LEFT 65% entirely empty dark charcoal, top 25% empty, bottom 25% quiet for cards. The most important property is delicate pencil lines and extensive negative space. Very subtle side illumination: left of paper almost black, right a little warmer and lighter. No interface or typography.
